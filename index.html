What you can do in the game

  • Create a character and start with either a "Nepo" or a "Lapo" life.
  • Find work, earn virtual naira and keep up with rent.
  • Meet, chat and work alongside other players in the same city.
  • No download or app store needed.

How it works

  1. Open the game. Tap "Play Lagos Life" above. It opens on the official site, so there's nothing to install.
  2. Create your character. The game gives you a starting life: Nepo, with money and connections, or Lapo, with fewer resources and microloans to lean on.
  3. Get a job. Work to earn virtual naira.
  4. Keep up with rent. Spend your naira on bills and on getting around the city's places.
  5. Meet other players. Everyone shares the same world, so you can chat and work alongside them.

Lagos Life questions

What is Lagos Life?

Lagos Life is a free life simulation game that runs in your browser. You build a character, work, earn virtual naira and pay rent in a version of Lagos inspired by the real city. It takes cues from The Sims 3 and from the Nigerian game Lagos Run.

Is Lagos Life free?

Yes. It's free to play and you don't need to download anything or install an app.

Who made Lagos Life?

Nigerian developer Shalom Rayhamen. It launched on October 1, 2026, which is Nigeria's Independence Day, and passed one million players within days.

What is Nepo vs Lapo?

They are the two starting lives in the game. Nepo is a life helped by money and connections. Lapo is a harder start with limited resources and microloans to lean on.

Is the money in the game real?

No. The naira you earn and spend only exists inside the game. The official site describes Lagos Life as a work of fiction made for entertainment.

Can I play with other people?

Yes. It's a multiplayer world where players can meet, chat and work in the same city.
